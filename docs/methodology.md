# Consulting methodology

The diagnostic begins with strategic context and a precise problem definition. It creates hypotheses before gathering evidence, evaluates evidence for and against candidate causes, distinguishes correlation from causation, compares options, recommends an intervention and translates it into a gated roadmap.

Function assessment covers strategy, finance, operations, sales, marketing, service, HR, technology, data and AI, risk and compliance, and supply chain. Maturity uses the five-level Initial–Optimised scale. Issues are organised MECE where practical, without forcing artificial separation where operational causes overlap.

Every recommendation states finding, evidence, root cause, intervention, benefit, delivery approach, risk controls and success measures. Benefits require a baseline, named owner, measurement cadence and Finance validation.
