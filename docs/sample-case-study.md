# NorthStar Retail Ltd — sample case study

NorthStar is a fictional UK retailer with 34 stores, an online channel, 620 employees and £48m annual revenue. The diagnostic uses synthetic monthly records designed to be internally consistent.

Delivery delay rises from 1.5 to 2.1 days (40%), while on-time performance declines from 91% to 73%. Stock-outs average 9.8% against a 4% target. Complaints rise from 820 to 1,190 per month and 61% carry delivery or availability tags. Weekly reporting consolidates 17 spreadsheets and consumes 96 staff-hours. Operating margin declines from 8.6% to 6.0%.

Recommended actions are automated executive reporting, inventory visibility, delivery control, a controlled demand-forecasting pilot, service recovery, store process standards and planner training. All benefits are indicative gross annual values. The roadmap releases scale funding only after KPI decision gates demonstrate improvement.
